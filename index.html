About Us

Precision Fastener Manufacturing

Al-Rehman Fasteners is a leading manufacturer and supplier of industrial-grade fastening solutions.

Quality Manufacturing, Reliable Supply

Al-Rehman Fasteners specializes in the manufacturing of high-quality nuts, bolts, nails, screws, washers, and anchor fasteners. Our facility is equipped with modern machinery to ensure precision and consistency in every product.

We serve a diverse range of industries including construction, infrastructure, automotive, furniture, and general manufacturing. Our commitment to quality and timely delivery has made us a trusted name in the fastener industry.

  • Modern manufacturing facility with precision machinery
  • Strict quality control at every production stage
  • Wide range of standard and custom fasteners
  • Competitive wholesale and bulk pricing
  • Reliable logistics and timely delivery
